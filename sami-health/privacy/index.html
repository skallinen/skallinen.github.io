Sami health privacy policy

Version 1, 29 October 2026.

Who uses it

Sami health is a personal tool. Its only user is its author, Sami Kallinen, who signs in with his own Google account.

What it reads

With read-only access granted on Google's consent screen, it reads steps, heart rate and resting heart rate, sleep and weight from the Google Health API. It writes nothing to Google.

Where the data goes

The data is stored in a database on the author's own home server and its encrypted backup. It is not sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for advertising, and it is not used to train any model.

Keeping and deleting

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account under Security, Third-party apps. The author can delete the stored data by deleting the database.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
