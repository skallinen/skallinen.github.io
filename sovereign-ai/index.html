AI sovereignty

Sovereign AI decision tree

Answer the questions from the top. Each end point shows how much control you get, what quality to expect, and what it costs.

29 October 2026. Fact-checked the same day against vendor pages, Artificial Analysis, Epoch AI, the CADA proposal and Finnish law. Costs marked "estimate" have no public price. What could not be confirmed is listed at the end.

Cost basis: one mid-sized organisation using about 350 million tokens a month (11 million a day, 80% input). Euros at 1 USD = 0.86 EUR, before VAT, no caching discounts. Double the volume and the API rows roughly double; the server rows stay flat until the hardware is full.

Whatever the branch

  • Put a gateway in front of every model. Access control, removal of personal data, an audit log, and the ability to switch model. Open source options cost little beyond setup.
  • Mix branches by data class. A for public work, B for ordinary personal data, F to H for security and legal files is a common, affordable combination.
  • Keep the way out open. Portable data, open interfaces and a tested exit plan matter more than the starting branch.
  • The chips are American in every branch. Even N3 runs on NVIDIA hardware. US export rules for Finland are currently unrestricted, but a replacement framework is still in draft.
  • The gap may shrink next week. Kimi K3.1 Pro's weights are promised by 4 November; if they ship, G and H gain a model about 13 points from the top instead of 27.
